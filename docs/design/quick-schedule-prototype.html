Quick Schedule

Paste a care plan from Fusion or open its PDF. You get a ready schedule that follows Missouri's rules, and you can change it within them.

Paste the care plan from Fusion

  1. In Fusion, open the participant's care plan.
  2. Select from the participant's name down to the last task, and copy.
  3. Paste it here. If Fusion shows the tasks on two pages, paste one page at a time.

No care plan handy?

Nothing you paste or open is uploaded or saved to our servers. It stays in this browser tab, and the participant's name and DCN are gone when you close it.

Checks

Unit math follows DHSS rules for 15-minute units: weekly frequency factors, short-month proration for daily tasks, and whole units only. A change that would break a rule isn't accepted; you'll see the limit instead. Checks marked "Needs review" use a rule DHSS hasn't confirmed yet, so they never count as passing.
